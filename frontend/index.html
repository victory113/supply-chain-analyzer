How this works

Step 1 — You upload your CSV
Your supply chain data is read client-side and sent to the FastAPI backend. Nothing is stored permanently.
Step 2 — Data is formatted into a structured prompt
The backend converts your CSV rows to JSON and injects them into a carefully engineered prompt that instructs Claude on exactly what to look for and how to respond.
Step 3 — Claude AI analyzes the data
The prompt is sent to Anthropic's Claude API. The model reasons over your data and returns structured JSON — risk titles, severity levels, affected items, and recommendations.
Step 4 — Results are rendered in real time
The frontend parses the JSON and renders risk cards, color-coded by severity, with plain-English explanations designed for non-technical stakeholders.
